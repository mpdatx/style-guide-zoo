Rewrite the passage in your own words.

You have no style guide and no house rules for this task. Do not adopt a named
style, and do not imitate the source's period, register, or sentence rhythm.
Write it the way you would write it if someone handed you this passage and
asked you to put it in your own words for a general reader.

Keep every fact, claim, step, and qualification the passage contains. Change
what is said in no way at all — only how it is said.
