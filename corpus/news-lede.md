The Fairhaven City Council voted Tuesday night to approve an amended version of the parks and recreation line item, following a meeting that also touched on the long-delayed replacement of the Third Street bridge. According to a spokesperson for the mayor's office, the revised figure reflects "ongoing conversations" among council members, though the spokesperson declined to characterize those conversations further. A separate memo, attributed by two council staffers to the finance subcommittee, put the adjustment at approximately $340,000, a figure that a budget analyst who reviewed the memo said was "in the range we expected." The bridge project, which has appeared on the capital plan since at least 2019, was not discussed in detail. Council member Dana Okafor, reached after the meeting, said only that "the process worked the way it's supposed to work, and I think residents should feel good about that."
