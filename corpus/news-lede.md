The Millbrook Hollow City Council voted Tuesday night to approve an amended version of the parks and recreation line item, following a meeting that also touched on the long-delayed replacement of the Third Street bridge. According to a spokesperson for the mayor's office, the revised figure reflects "ongoing conversations" among council members, though the spokesperson declined to characterize those conversations further. A separate memo, attributed by two council staffers to the finance subcommittee, put the adjustment at approximately $340,000, a figure that a budget analyst who reviewed the memo said was "in the range we expected." Buried near the end of that same memo, and not raised by any council member during the meeting, was a line noting that the bridge's lead structural engineer had flagged a load-bearing crack serious enough to warrant closing the span to truck traffic within 90 days. The bridge project, which has appeared on the capital plan since at least 2019 and has already drawn 14 change orders, was not discussed in detail. Council member Dana Okafor, reached after the meeting, said only that "the process worked the way it's supposed to work, and I think residents should feel good about that."
