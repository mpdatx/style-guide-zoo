Operators must ensure the guard is lowered to within the recommended clearance of the workpiece before the blade is engaged, although the tensioning wheel should be checked periodically throughout a shift regardless of guard position, since drift in tension can occur even when the machine has not been used continuously. The lower blade guide must be inspected for wear whenever the blade has been changed, unless the replacement blade is of the same width and pitch as the one it replaces, in which case a visual check may be sufficient provided the operator has completed the tensioning procedure described elsewhere in this manual. Loose clothing and jewelry should not be worn near the machine, and long hair must be tied back. Operators may leave the work area only after the blade has come to a complete stop, though the machine's residual heat can persist for several minutes and caution is advised.
